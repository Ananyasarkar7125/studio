# **App Name**: Reflectly

## Core Features:

- Daily Journal Entry: Freeform text area for users to record daily thoughts and experiences.
- Mood Tracking: A simple mood selector (e.g., happy, neutral, sad) for users to log their daily mood.
- Calendar View: Display journal entries and mood logs on a calendar for easy review.
- Trend Summary: Generate a summary of mood trends over time, highlighting patterns and fluctuations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft green (#A7D1AB), evoking calmness and growth.
- Background color: Desaturated light green (#E4EEE5).
- Accent color: Earthy brown (#B38B6D), providing warmth and stability.
- Body and headline font: 'Literata', a transitional serif with a literary and slightly vintage feel.
- Use minimalist, nature-inspired icons for mood selections and navigation.
- Clean, uncluttered design with ample white space to promote a sense of calm.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user experience without being distracting.
